AM2ARM Launcher for ARM64 Linux — Beta 1
Game version 1.6.3

1. Extract the launcher ZIP on your computer. Open the AM2ARM folder
   beside this readme and put your own Windows AM2R 1.1 ZIP inside it.
   Keep the game ZIP zipped. For example, AM2ARM/AM2R_11.zip works on
   every supported device. You can keep the ZIP's existing filename.
2. Go back to the folder containing this readme. Copy its contents directly
   into the game library, merging with the folders already there.
   On a two-card handheld, use the games card.
   Use a card reader or your firmware's file transfer and keep 3 GB free
   after copying.

   Where to copy the extracted contents
     Stock Anbernic RG DS       Top level of ROMS after first boot
     KNULLI                     SHARE volume > roms after first boot
     NextUI, stock TrimUI,      Top level of the games card
       spruceOS
     AmberELEC                  Top level of GAMES after first boot
     dArkOS (BATLEXP G350)      Top level of EASYROMS after first boot
     muOS (TrimUI Brick)        Top level of the games card after Install
     ROCKNIX                    games-internal/roms on the system card, roms
                                on a games card, or the games-roms share

   For example, on a KNULLI card you will see roms/AM2ARM/AM2R_11.zip
   and roms/ports/AM2ARM.sh on SHARE.
3. Open AM2ARM from Ports, Tools (NextUI) or Apps (stock TrimUI and spruceOS).
   On muOS, open Explore Content > Ports. The Brick was checked on exFAT.
   On case-sensitive ext4, copy roms/ports/AM2ARM.sh into ROMS/Ports/.
   Choose Standard or Dual Screen with the D-pad and A. Both are available
   on your first visit. Standard uses one screen. Dual Screen puts gameplay
   on one display and the map on the other. The launcher highlights a
   suggestion. Choose Install to patch that edition from your ZIP.
   Play opens an edition already installed on the card.
   On firmware with a one-output display driver, you can install Dual
   Screen for another setup and play Standard on the handheld.
   If the launcher needs help finding your ZIP, the file picker opens.
   Choose the original with the D-pad and A. B returns to the edition menu.
4. Open AM2ARM again to go straight into the last edition you played.
   To switch editions, hold D-pad Up as AM2ARM opens. You can also
   remove AM2ARM-data/edition.txt from the card to reopen the menu.
   Keep your original ZIP for repairs or to install the other edition.

If the entry is missing on ROCKNIX, check that ports/AM2ARM.sh and
am2arm-launcher/launch.sh are in the same game library, then open Start >
Game Settings > Update Gamelists.

The launcher includes the display files for the TrimUI Brick. The launcher
ZIP and your own Windows AM2R 1.1 ZIP are enough for offline installation.
If a display file is missing or damaged, extract the launcher ZIP again.

New installs and your edition choice are in AM2ARM-data. A complete 1.6.3
installation in a recognized location opens directly. Back up saves through
Options > Data before changing versions. To exit, save, then hold Start +
Select for one second. To report a problem, include your model, firmware
and am2arm-launcher/AM2ARM/logs/compatibility.txt.
